Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Offerpedia ("Offerpedia", "we", "us") provides tools that let merchants create an online store and receive order notifications on WhatsApp. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website, our merchant dashboard and related services (the "Service").

1. Who we are

Offerpedia, CR No. 126828-1, Kingdom of Bahrain. For any privacy question, contact Hello@offerpedia.org or call +973 3921 7883.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, mobile number, store name and a hashed password.
  • Store and order data: products, prices, orders, and the names, phone numbers and delivery areas of your customers, which you add or which are provided when customers place orders.
  • Messaging data: the contents and delivery status of order notifications sent through WhatsApp.
  • Usage and device data: pages visited, browser type, IP address and approximate location, collected through standard logs and cookies.
  • Communications: messages you send us for support.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and operate your store.
  • To process orders and send order notifications and delivery updates.
  • To provide support, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep the Service secure.
  • To understand how the Service is used and improve it.
  • To send service announcements and, where permitted, marketing messages you can opt out of at any time.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal basis

We process personal data to perform our contract with you, with your consent where required, to meet legal obligations, and for our legitimate interests in running and securing the Service, in line with Bahrain's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 30 of 2018).

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Service, such as hosting, analytics and messaging providers (including WhatsApp / Meta for notification delivery), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities where required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.
  • A successor in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, with notice to you.

Merchants are responsible for their own customers' data they collect through their store, and Offerpedia processes that data on the merchant's behalf.

6. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside Bahrain. Where this happens we take steps to ensure your data receives an adequate level of protection.

7. Retention

We keep personal data while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the Service, meet legal and accounting obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

8. Security

We use passwords stored as one-way hashes, encrypted connections and access controls. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and sign out on shared devices.

9. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in, remember preferences and measure usage. You can clear or block them in your browser settings, though parts of the Service may stop working.

10. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, and ask for a copy of your data. To make a request, email Hello@offerpedia.org. We will respond within a reasonable time and may need to verify your identity.

11. Data deletion

To delete your account and associated data, email Hello@offerpedia.org from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my data". We will confirm once it is complete, except for records we must keep by law.

12. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version, and we will notify you of material changes.
